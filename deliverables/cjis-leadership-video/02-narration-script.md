# Narration script

133 spoken words. Read it calmly, at a pace that lands between 55 and 65 seconds. Leave a short breath between the paragraphs. The picture timing is in `03-shot-list.md`.

Say “see-jis” for CJIS. Say “inactive,” even though the screen spells it InActive. The script does not speak the acronyms printed on the form.

---

City staff need a dependable way to see who is cleared for work that depends on criminal justice information systems.

The CJIS Applicant Tracker keeps one record for each applicant. It follows that clearance from the first information received, through fingerprints and security awareness training, to the certification dates and the next inquiry, five years later.

People who only need a status can look it up. People who keep the records can open one applicant and see whether that clearance is active.

Across the top, anyone signed in can see how many applicants are active, how many are not, and whose five-year inquiry is due.

Each update stays with the record, so there is a history of what changed and when.

One shared record. A clear status. One place to follow the clearance.

---

## If the read runs long

Drop the closing line to: “One shared record. A clear status.”

Leave the middle paragraphs in place. They carry who the tool helps and why the dates matter.

## Leave these lines out

- Any number of hours, dollars, or errors.
- “Now live,” “citywide,” or “spreadsheets are gone.”
- “Compliant,” “encrypted,” or “connected to NCIC.”
- The sign-in steps, the role codes, and anything found under Settings.

## Product definition

`docs/product/product-definition.md` is the authoritative statement of purpose, intended users, outcomes, scope, and explicit exclusions. It requires product owner approval (G-SCOPE) before implementation sprints begin.

## Requirements

- Work MUST trace to an approved product outcome via the active sprint or change record.
- Acceptance criteria MUST be testable and recorded before work is authorized.
- Scope changes are C2 minimum and require product owner approval; outcome changes require the business/outcome owner's recorded acceptance decision.
- Outcome acceptance authority is separate from implementation review and MUST be recorded by the designated business/outcome owner.
- Labeling work as an experiment, prototype, configuration, or maintenance task does not exempt it from these rules.
